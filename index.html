How it works

  • Import statements. Choose one or more bank or credit card statements (PDF, CSV or Excel) from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, American Express and more. Password-protected PDFs work.

  • Optional: bank alert emails. Connect one or more Gmail (or Outlook) accounts with read-only access. Kittuni looks only at emails from your banks' alert addresses and turns them into transactions between statements.

  • See the picture. Monthly and financial-year spending, categories, card bill reminders, CSV export.

Private by design

  • Everything is read and stored on your iPhone. There is no Kittuni server and no Kittuni account.

  • Email access goes directly from your phone to Google or Microsoft. The developer never sees your email or your transactions.

  • On supported iPhones, optional AI uses only Apple's on-device model: nothing is sent anywhere, Apple's Private Cloud Compute is not used, and AI never changes a category you set.

  • No ads, analytics or tracking. Read-only email access: Kittuni can't send, delete or change mail.

  • Disconnect email or delete all data any time from inside the app.

Kittuni's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Kittuni isn't

Kittuni is personal bookkeeping. It doesn't move money, log in to your bank, give financial advice, offer loans or check credit scores.
