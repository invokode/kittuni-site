Kittuni

Every rupee, sorted. Right on your iPhone.

Kittuni turns your bank statements and alert emails into a clear picture of where your money goes: categorised, budgeted and searchable. No sign-up, and nothing leaves your phone.

Kittuni is made by Sarath Krishna Prasad, an independent developer in Hyderabad, India.

Sample data on a simulated iPhone.

Works with

PDF, CSV and Excel statements from major Indian banks and card issuers, including password-protected PDFs; bank alerts from one or more Gmail accounts.

Import statements in seconds.

Drop in a statement and Kittuni finds every transaction, so you can skip typing them in one by one.

Unlock password-protected PDFs. Enter the password once and Kittuni can remember it securely on this iPhone, so next month's statement opens without asking.

Bank alerts from your inbox.

Connect one or more Gmail accounts and spending shows up as it happens. Kittuni won't count a transaction twice when the statement arrives later.

Automatic categories.

Rent, groceries, travel, EMIs and more are sorted for you. Create your own categories too, like Pets or Kids.

Budgets that keep you honest.

Set an overall monthly budget or one per category, and see at a glance how much you have left.

See your month at a glance.

A single chart shows where the money went, with month and year views and easy comparisons.

Recurring payments in one place.

Subscriptions, SIPs, EMIs and yearly renewals are listed together, so nothing surprises you.

Tags, splits and search.

Split a mixed bill across categories, tag a trip or a festival, and find any transaction instantly.

Savings goals.

Set a target and follow the progress, for an emergency fund, a trip, or anything else you are putting money aside for.

A look ahead.

See where your balance is heading, along with upcoming bills and what is left to spend each day.

On-device AI

On supported iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Kittuni uses Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Ask your money. Ask in plain English, like "How much did I spend on food this month?", and get an answer worked out from your own transactions.
  • Your month, summarised. A short, readable recap of what you spent and received, and how it compares with last month.
  • Smarter categories. For transactions Kittuni can't place on its own, it suggests a category and a cleaner merchant name. Accept a suggestion with a tap, or let Kittuni apply them automatically.

Everything else in Kittuni works without Apple Intelligence.

Encrypted backups.

Save a password-protected backup to Files and restore it on a new iPhone whenever you need to.

Private by design

  • Your data stays on your iPhone.

    Transactions, budgets and categories are stored on your device.

  • No account.

    There's nothing to sign up for. Just open the app and start.

  • No servers.

    Kittuni has no backend. When you connect Gmail, your iPhone talks to Google directly, and only to read your bank alert emails.

  • No tracking.

    No ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers. Data Not Collected.
