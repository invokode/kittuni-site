Kittuni Support

Help, subscriptions and your data

Need help? Email kittuni.app@gmail.com. I'm one developer and usually reply within two working days (IST). When you write, please include your iPhone model, iOS version and Kittuni version (Settings › About in Kittuni). Please don't attach real statements or screenshots that show account numbers.

Getting started

  • Do I need an account? No. Open Kittuni and start; there's nothing to sign up for.
  • Where is my data? On your iPhone, in Kittuni's storage. It isn't uploaded to me or to any cloud database.
  • Which banks work? Kittuni reads PDF, CSV and Excel statements from major Indian banks and card issuers. See the full list in Kittuni under Settings › Supported banks. Other banks usually work too; Kittuni checks each statement's balances and flags anything it isn't sure about.
  • My statement PDF has a password. Enter it when Kittuni asks. You can let Kittuni remember it on this iPhone (in the Keychain, on this device only) so next month's statement opens automatically.
  • Kittuni didn't read my statement correctly. Check the preview before importing and fix anything flagged. If your bank isn't supported yet, see below.

Help Kittuni support your bank

In Kittuni, open Settings › Supported banks › Help Kittuni support your bank (or use the same button when an import fails). Kittuni makes an anonymised text copy of the statement's layout, shows it to you, and opens an email to me in the Mail app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. It's optional, you must be 18 or older, and Kittuni works the same either way, Pro included.

  • To delete what you sent or withdraw consent: email kittuni.app@gmail.com from the same address with the subject "Withdraw consent". I'll stop using it and delete it within 30 days. I also delete shared layouts once support for that bank is built.
  • Details are in the privacy policy, section 2.

Kittuni Pro and subscriptions

  • Prices: Yearly is ₹499 a year with a 7-day free trial. Monthly is ₹99 a month, with your first month for ₹29. The offers are for new subscribers, one per Apple Account. The free version imports unlimited statements and shows your last 3 months.
  • Renewal: after the free trial, Yearly renews at ₹499 a year. After the ₹29 first month, Monthly renews at ₹99 a month. Both renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period (including the trial or first month) ends.
  • Cancel or change plan: on your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kittuni.
  • Restore purchases: in Kittuni, open Settings › Kittuni Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backups and moving to a new iPhone

In Kittuni, Settings › Backup › Export creates a password-protected backup file. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then use Restore on the new iPhone. I can't recover a forgotten backup password.

Privacy and deleting your data

  • Everything on your iPhone: in Kittuni, Settings › Delete everything, or delete the app.
  • Anything you sent me (shared layouts or emails): email kittuni.app@gmail.com from the address you used, asking for a copy, correction or deletion. I'll reply within 30 days.
  • Full details, including your rights under India's DPDP Act: privacy policy.
